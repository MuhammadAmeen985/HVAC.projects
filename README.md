# HVAC Projects | Industrial Utilities & Mechanical Engineering

## Overview

Welcome to my HVAC Projects repository. This repository is intended to document practical knowledge, engineering projects, technical calculations, maintenance practices, troubleshooting methods, and energy-efficiency concepts related to Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) and industrial utility systems.

My interests combine hands-on industrial experience with mechanical engineering, thermal systems, equipment reliability, automation, and AI-based predictive maintenance.

## Purpose

The main objectives of this repository are to:

- Document industrial HVAC and utility equipment.
- Organize equipment specifications, operating principles, and maintenance procedures.
- Record troubleshooting methods and practical engineering observations.
- Support engineering calculations related to thermal systems, airflow, pressure, and energy consumption.
- Explore ways to improve equipment reliability and energy efficiency.
- Develop future smart-HVAC solutions using IoT sensors, data analysis, and machine learning.

## Future Development: Smart HVAC and Predictive Maintenance

An area of future development is the integration of HVAC systems with Internet of Things (IoT) sensors and data-driven monitoring.

Potential applications include:

- Monitoring temperature, pressure, vibration, and power consumption
- Identifying unusual operating conditions
- Analyzing trends in equipment performance
- Exploring machine-learning methods for fault detection
- Supporting condition-based maintenance and energy optimization

These are development goals, not claims of implemented functionality unless supported by working prototypes and test results.

## Repository Structure

As the project documentation grows, files may be organized into the following folders:

- `equipment/` — equipment descriptions and specifications
- `maintenance/` — maintenance checklists and procedures

## Safety and Documentation

Industrial maintenance must follow site procedures, equipment manuals, applicable standards, and authorized safety practices. Do not publish confidential workplace documents, personal information, proprietary drawings, or sensitive plant data.

Engineering calculations and recommendations should include their assumptions, units, operating conditions, and relevant limitations.
